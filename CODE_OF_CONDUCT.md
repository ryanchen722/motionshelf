# Code of Conduct / 行為準則

We want MotionShelf spaces (this repository and the community Discord) to be friendly and safe for everyone.
我們希望這個專案和社群，對每個人都友善、安全。

## Expected behavior / 期待的行為

- Be respectful and patient with others, especially beginners. 尊重並耐心對待他人，尤其是新手。
- Give and accept feedback kindly. 友善地給予和接受意見。
- Credit other people's work and respect its license. 標明他人作品的來源，並尊重其授權。

## Not acceptable / 不被接受的行為

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, or hateful, sexual or violent content. 騷擾、辱罵、歧視，或仇恨、色情、暴力內容。
- Publishing someone else's personal information. 公開他人的個人資料。
- Sharing effects or code that is malicious, deceptive, or stolen. 分享惡意、誤導或盜用的效果或程式碼。
- Spam and unrequested advertising. 垃圾訊息與未經同意的廣告。

## Enforcement / 處理方式

Maintainers may remove content, warn, mute or ban people who break these rules. For serious violations (malicious effects, stolen work, harassment) the ban is permanent.
維護者可以刪除內容、警告、禁言或移出違規的人；嚴重違規（惡意效果、盜用、騷擾）會直接永久移出。

To report a problem, open a private channel with a maintainer on Discord, or use GitHub's "Report content" on the item.
要回報問題，可以在 Discord 私訊維護者，或使用 GitHub 的「Report content」。
